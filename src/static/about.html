About

Who we are & our mission

We are a team of AI enthusiasts and engineers dedicated to simplifying document processing. Our mission is to make optical character recognition and full-text search accessible, fast, and completely automated. Designed with a passion for beautiful, dynamic interfaces and cutting-edge backend technology.

What is OCR? Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a groundbreaking technology that identifies and extracts text from images, scanned documents, and PDFs. It transforms static pixels into searchable, editable, and fully digital text, enabling blazing-fast document analysis and data retrieval.

Built for the AWS Cloud Clash project. Powered by FastAPI, Tesseract OCR, and a robust persistent search engine.
